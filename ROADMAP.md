# Roadmap

Updated 23 September 2026. These are priorities, not promised dates or delivered features.

## Available now

Free MIT local agent 0.2.0-preview.0, versioned downloads and checksums, local MCP mode, the optional outbound managed relay, pairing/revocation, and 13 file/process tools. [Installation](INSTALL.md) explains the current limits.

## Under review

Ganado Bridge 0.2.0 is submitted to the OpenAI directory. Review does not guarantee approval or publication. Keep the submitted tool contract stable while resolving any actual review findings; do not describe the listing as publicly available before publication.

## Next evidence to earn

Make independent installation, a useful first task, clear error handling and a second real use easier. Separate download requests and synthetic checks from verified human activity. Improve the Mac setup around observed friction, not a second chatbot interface.

A signed/notarized installer, menu-bar status, automatic startup, Windows/Linux product support and a commercial SLA are **not currently shipped**. They require separate implementation and acceptance before being advertised.

## Commercial services

The local agent remains MIT. No managed subscription is sold today. Any future paid hosted service must provide demonstrated additional value and clear delivery, billing, cancellation and support terms. A speculative monthly price is not an offer.
